Safety sweep: practice activities

Three short checks that go with the walkthrough. Each one can be retried as often as you like — nothing is recorded.

If you haven't walked the apartment yet, start there: First Visit: The Safety Sweep.

Activity 1

What was out of scope?

A real visit note, written at the end of a shift. Four things in it are outside a non-licensed caregiver's role. Click the word that names each one, then check your answers.

Activity 2

Fix it, ask first, or report it?

Nine findings from the walkthrough. Click a finding to select it, then click Place here under the response it calls for. Click a placed finding to send it back.

Fix it now

Reversible, inside your lane, nothing of theirs changes.

Ask first

Their home, their call, before anything moves.

Report it

Needs a nurse, therapist, supervisor or landlord.

Activity 3

Kitchen check: where does forgetting show?

First visit, kitchen. One thing here tells you most about whether cooking is being forgotten — scorched pans, a cloth left over a handle, post stacked on it. Click it.
